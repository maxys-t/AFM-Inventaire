Import photos

The file name decides what the photo covers, from the most precise to the widest:
SYN-001.jpg one item · Roland Juno-106.jpg a model of one brand · Juno-106.jpg a model · Cordial XLR.jpg one brand across a whole sub-category · XLR.jpg a whole sub-category.
The most precise file always wins: drop Cordial XLR.jpg and XLR 3m.jpg together and the 3 m cables keep their own photo. Images are cropped to a square and resized automatically.

Crop photo

Drag the image and adjust the zoom — the visible square is what gets saved.
